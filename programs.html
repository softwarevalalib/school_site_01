Our Programs

We run from Pre-Nursery to 7th Grade

🧸

Pre-Nursery & Nursery

Ages 2-4 years

Our early childhood education program provides a warm, nurturing environment where your child's first educational experiences are positive and enriching. We focus on developing foundational skills through play-based learning and age-appropriate activities.

What We Offer:

  • Play-based learning approach
  • Motor skills development (fine and gross)
  • Basic literacy and numeracy introduction
  • Social skills and emotional development
  • Creative arts and music
  • Safe and colorful learning environment
  • Individualized attention from caring teachers

Learning Outcomes:

By the end of this program, students will have developed basic social skills, improved communication abilities, enhanced motor skills, and a love for learning that prepares them for primary education.

📚

Primary School (1st - 5th Grade)

Ages 5-10 years

Our primary school program builds strong academic foundations while nurturing each child's unique talents and interests. We provide a comprehensive curriculum that balances academic excellence with character development.

Core Subjects:

  • English Language and Literacy
  • Mathematics
  • Science (Basic and Elementary)
  • Social Studies
  • Religious and Moral Education
  • Physical Education
  • Creative Arts (Music, Drawing, Crafts)
  • Computer Studies

Special Features:

  • Small class sizes for personalized attention
  • Experienced and dedicated teachers
  • Interactive learning methods
  • Regular assessments and progress reports
  • Extra-curricular activities
  • Character and moral education
  • Library access and reading programs

Learning Outcomes:

Students develop strong literacy and numeracy skills, critical thinking abilities, creativity, and positive character traits that prepare them for advanced learning.

🎯

Junior Secondary (6th - 7th Grade)

Ages 11-13 years

Our junior secondary program prepares students for the transition to full secondary education. We provide advanced instruction in core subjects while developing leadership skills and independent thinking.

Advanced Curriculum:

  • Advanced English Language and Literature
  • Advanced Mathematics (Pre-Algebra concepts)
  • Integrated Science (Biology, Chemistry, Physics basics)
  • Social Studies and Civics
  • Religious Studies
  • Information and Communication Technology
  • Physical and Health Education
  • Creative and Cultural Arts

Skills Development:

  • Critical thinking and problem-solving
  • Research and project work
  • Public speaking and presentation skills
  • Leadership development
  • Team collaboration
  • Time management and study skills
  • Exam preparation techniques

Learning Outcomes:

Students are well-prepared for secondary school entrance exams and beyond, with strong academic foundations, developed study habits, and the confidence to tackle advanced education challenges.
