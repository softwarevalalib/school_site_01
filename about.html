About Lorada Scholars Academy

Where Learning Meets Excellence!

Our Story

Lorada Scholars Academy is a premier educational institution committed to providing quality education from Pre-Nursery through 7th Grade. We believe in nurturing young minds and building futures through comprehensive, value-based education.

Our Mission

To provide exceptional educational experiences that develop the whole child - academically, socially, emotionally, and spiritually. We strive to create an environment where every student can discover their potential and develop the skills needed for lifelong success.

Our Motto

"Nurturing Minds, Building Future"

Our Foundation

"Train up the child in the way he should go; and when he is old, he will not depart from it."

This biblical principle guides our approach to education, emphasizing character development alongside academic excellence.

Our Values

📚

Quality Education

We maintain high academic standards and use proven teaching methodologies to ensure every student receives excellence in education.

👨‍🏫

Experienced Faculty

Our dedicated team of experienced educators are passionate about teaching and committed to student success.

🎯

Holistic Development

We focus on developing the whole child - intellectually, physically, emotionally, and spiritually.

🛡️

Safe & Secure Environment

We provide a protected, nurturing environment where children can learn and grow with confidence.
