Admission Process

  1. Contact the school via phone or visit our campus
  2. Complete and submit application form
  3. Submit required documents
  4. Schedule an interview/assessment (for certain grades)
  5. Receive admission decision
  6. Complete enrollment and payment

Required Documents

  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Recent passport photographs (2)
  • Previous school report card (if applicable)
  • Transfer certificate (for students from other schools)
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Parent/Guardian valid ID
